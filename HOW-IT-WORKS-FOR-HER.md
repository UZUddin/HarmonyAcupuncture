# How the Booking Website Works (Simple Guide)

## The short version
Your website checks your **Google Calendar** to see when you're free.
When someone books online, it adds the appointment to your calendar —
just like if you'd typed it in yourself.

## Two ways to manage your schedule

**Option A — the Schedule Helper (simplest)**
Go to your own website (hatcm.com), scroll all the way to the bottom,
and click **"Staff"**. Enter your passcode once — after that, your
browser remembers it, so you won't need to type it again on that same
phone or computer.

You'll see your week laid out day by day. Tap "+ Add busy time" on any
day, pick a start and end time from a dropdown, and it's done —
automatically shows up on your real Google Calendar too, and disappears
from the booking website.

You can also see your patient appointments there (marked "Patient"), and
reschedule or cancel them directly — the patient gets notified
automatically if they opted into email reminders when booking.

**Option B — Google Calendar directly**
If you're already comfortable with the Google Calendar app, you can keep
using it exactly as before. Anything you add there shows up in the
Schedule Helper too — they're always in sync, since they're really just
two windows into the same calendar.

Use whichever one feels easier. There's no wrong choice, and you can
switch between them anytime.

## What you need to do day-to-day
**Nothing new — but one habit matters.** The website only knows about
time you've actually put on your calendar (through either option above).
If lunch with a friend isn't blocked, the website has no way to know and
will let people book that time.

**So: add it to your calendar, even with just a simple title like
"Lunch" or "Busy."** You don't need to write any details.

A few specifics:

- **Default hours are 9am–6pm (Mon–Fri) and 9am–1pm (Saturday).** If your
  calendar is empty during those hours, the *whole* day shows as
  bookable. You only need to add events for the times you're NOT
  available.
- **One-time change** (lunch with a friend this specific Tuesday) → add
  a normal one-time event for that time.
- **Regular thing** (you always take lunch 12–1, or never work past 4pm
  on Fridays) → create the event **once** and set it to repeat
  daily/weekly. Google Calendar has a "Does not repeat" dropdown when
  you create an event — change it to "Daily" or "Weekly" and you'll
  never have to add it again.
- **Important (only if using Google Calendar directly, Option B):** when
  you create an event there, make sure it's NOT set to "Free" (there's a
  "Busy / Free" option in the event details). Busy is the normal default.
  The Schedule Helper (Option A) always creates events as Busy
  automatically, so this only matters if you're adding things straight
  in Google Calendar.

## The one thing you'll do during setup (not after)
When we first connect the website to your calendar, Google will show you
a screen asking "Allow this app to see your calendar?" You'll click
**Allow** once. That's it — a single click, one time, and then it's done.

## If you ever want to change your regular hours
(For example: "I'm not working Fridays anymore" or "I want to open
earlier on Saturdays.") Just message [your name] — that's a quick code
change, not something you need to do yourself.

## What you don't need to worry about
- No new account to create or remember a password for (beyond your
  existing Google account)
- No admin dashboard to learn
- No separate place where "bookings" live — they're just events on your
  calendar, right alongside everything else
